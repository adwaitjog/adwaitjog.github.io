About

I am an Associate Professor (with tenure) and Anita Jones Faculty Fellow in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Virginia. My research lies in the broad area of computer architecture and systems, with a special focus on GPUs and memory systems. I lead the Insight Computer Architecture Lab.
