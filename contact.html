Prospective Students

We are always looking for motivated students interested in computer architecture and systems, especially GPUs, memory systems, and secure and reliable computing.

How to Apply

{% include icon.html name="cap" %}Ph.D. Applicants

Please apply through the UVA Computer Science Ph.D. program and mention your interest in the Insight Computer Architecture Lab led by Prof. Jog in your application.

{% include icon.html name="flask" %}Current UVA Students

Send an email with your CV, transcript, and a brief description of your research interests.

Collaborations

{% include icon.html name="handshake" %}Academic and Industry Partners

We welcome collaborations with academic and industry partners. For research and technical collaborations or sponsored projects, please reach out by email with details about your organization and the proposed work.
