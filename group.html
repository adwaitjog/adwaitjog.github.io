Insight Computer Architecture Lab

We study how to build faster, more efficient, and more trustworthy computer systems. Our goal is to generate the insights needed to architect future computers that are more energy-efficient, capable, reliable, and secure.

Research

We work across computer architecture, with a special focus on the performance, reliability, and security of GPUs, heterogeneous systems, and accelerators. Our approach pairs careful workload characterization with new architectural mechanisms and fast, accurate modeling tools. Our work spans:
